The problem

Students are being recruited as money mules.

Criminals offer "easy money" for letting funds pass through a bank account. Many students don't realise it's money laundering. Today's systems usually spot it after the money has gone, and by then the student is the one left with a closed account or a criminal record.

How it works

Four steps, in the gap between in and out.

  1. 01

    Stream

    Payments flow into LinuxONE, where the bank's transactions already live. No data leaves the platform.

  2. 02

    Score

    Our XGBoost model scores each payment on 24 point-in-time signals (new senders, how fast money is sent on, account age) in about a millisecond.

  3. 03

    Warn

    Above the student cut-off, the student sees a calm, plain-English prompt before they send the money on. The bank's analyst sees a wider set of alerts.

  4. 04

    Explain

    The analyst gets a case note built from the model's reasons, with the student's answers attached to the alert.
